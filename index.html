What the Fed talks about: topic modeling FOMC minutes

A pipeline that scrapes every Federal Open Market Committee meeting's minutes from 1993 to now, stores them in MongoDB, and uses LDA topic modeling to show what the Fed talked about over time. The Fed's website has used several layouts over the years, so there's a scraper for each era. Minutes are lemmatized with spaCy, filtered to nouns, stripped of stopwords and FOMC boilerplate, and joined into bigrams like balance_sheet and swap_arrangement before training a gensim LDA model. Each meeting's topic mix is saved back to Mongo, and saved models are kept by date so retraining never overwrites an older run.

I compared several settings and chose four topics. The topics were easier to tell apart and name, though this wasn't the setting with the highest coherence score.

TopicWhat it's about
Balance SheetAsset holdings, credit markets and financial stability, mostly after 2008
Currency OperationReports and votes on the Fed's trading in Treasuries and foreign currencies
Over-expansionA strong economy and rate hikes to cool it down
Economic SlowdownA weak economy, rate cuts and waiting for a recovery

A Streamlit dashboard shows each topic's share across all years. For any year it lists that year's meetings with their main topic and links to the minutes. Clicking a meeting shows its topic mix and opens a pyLDAvis map on its main topic.

Python · MongoDB · BeautifulSoup · spaCy · NLTK · gensim · pyLDAvis · Streamlit

Forecasting U.S. natural gas storage

Storage levels drive natural gas prices and the derivatives that track them, so a model that predicts them accurately and explainably is useful. Using the EIA's weekly storage data, we compared a seasonal ARIMA model against a Bayesian linear regression on lagged values that we fit with a hand-written Gibbs sampler in R. Storage falls every winter as heating demand rises, so a 52-week seasonal term does most of the work.

After hyperparameter tuning, the best SARIMA (26,0,5)×(2,1,2)52 reached an RMSE of 131 Bcf on held-out 2025–2026 data. The Bayesian model's RMSE was about 598 Bcf.

Python · statsmodels · R · Gibbs sampling · walk-forward CV

Am I Underwater? A portfolio tracker

A no-login stock portfolio tracker. Anyone can create a portfolio and bookmark its private link. Add holdings with shares and cost basis, and a live table shows each position's dollar and percentage return, with prices refreshing every 30 seconds from the Finnhub API. Data lives in SQLite on a persistent volume, and the app runs in Docker on Fly.io.

Node.js · Express · SQLite · Finnhub API · Docker · Fly.io
